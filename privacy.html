Privacy Policy

Digital Rewards respects your privacy and handles personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

When you fill in a form on this website we collect what you give us: your name, business name, business type, number of branches, email address, WhatsApp number and any message you add.

Why we collect it

We use your details to respond to your request, book and prepare your free growth assessment, and send you a proposal if you ask for one. We do not sell your information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run our business, such as hosting, email and messaging providers, and only as far as they need it to provide that service.

Cookies and analytics

If we use analytics or advertising cookies, we ask for your consent first. You can decline and still use the website.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to deal with your request and to meet our legal obligations, then delete them.

Your rights

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.
