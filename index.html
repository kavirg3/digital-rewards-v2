Questions Owners Ask Us.

What is the difference between Loyalty Software and Managed Growth?

Loyalty Software gives you the tools and you run the programme yourself. With Managed Growth our team plans, builds and runs it for you, then reports back every month.

Which one should I choose?

If you have time and like doing it yourself, start with the software. If you want it done properly without adding to your week, choose Managed Growth. The free assessment helps you decide.

Can I start with the software and move to Managed Growth later?

Yes. Your card, members and history stay the same. We simply take over running it.

Who builds and runs my programme?

With Managed Growth, our team does all of it. With Loyalty Software, you do, with our onboarding and academy to help.

Do my customers need an app?

No. The card is saved to the wallet already on their phone, where their device supports it. They join by scanning a QR code or tapping a link.

Does it work with Apple Wallet and Google Wallet?

Yes, where the customer's device supports it. We confirm this during setup.

How do I know if it is working?

We record a baseline before launch, then track sign-ups, repeat visits, rewards redeemed and campaign responses against it.

Can it work with my POS?

It depends on your POS. We check first. The programme can also run alongside your POS without an integration.
